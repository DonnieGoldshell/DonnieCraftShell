# Product

## Purpose

DonnieCraftShell helps Path of Exile 2 players make economically informed crafting decisions. The product should answer whether an item is worth crafting further or should be sold immediately.

## Long-Term Modules

- **Craft Advisor**: evaluates an existing item and recommends the next economically optimal crafting action.
- **Profit Finder**: searches for crafting strategies with attractive expected profit, ROI, and risk-adjusted returns.
- **Meta & Modifiers**: maps desirable modifiers to item types, skills, classes, ascendancies, and build archetypes.
- **Economy**: tracks exchange rates and prices for Divine Orbs, Exalted Orbs, Omens, Essences, and related materials.

## First MVP Scope

The first MVP supports only rare Quivers. Bows, rings, amulets, armour, and other item classes are intentionally out of scope until the Quiver workflow proves the architecture.

## Non-Goals For Now

- No full crafting simulator.
- No broad item-class support.
- No unverified PoE2 rule assumptions.
- No automated trading or market manipulation workflows.

All PoE2-specific rules, modifier data, pricing sources, and API capabilities must be marked `TODO / NEEDS VERIFICATION` until confirmed by reliable references.
